3. Manually verified pronunciation facts: Cambridge Dictionary (Cambridge
University Press & Assessment), Collins English Dictionary (HarperCollins), and
Oxford Learner's Dictionaries (Oxford University Press), and
SpanishDictionary.com (Dictionary Media Group, Inc., a division of IXL Learning).
Only short pronunciation transcriptions are recorded, with the exact source
page and verification date in content/verified-phonetic-supplements.json.
Context-specific homograph corrections are in content/verified-word-phonetic-overrides.json.
No dictionary definitions, example sentences or publisher audio are copied.
These entries are not asserted to be CC BY-SA or GPL licensed.
SpanishDictionary.com references use each complete phrase's own UK/US
International Phonetic Alphabet rows, not separate word entries or its SPA row.
Formatting changes: collapse layout whitespace; enclose transcriptions in slashes;
for be careful, represent the source's bold stressed syllable with IPA stress
mark and its syllable hyphen with a dot. Original vowels and consonants are
preserved; no additional vowel lengths or sentence stress are inferred.
